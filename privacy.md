# Nexus Guardian — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Nexus Guardian ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that provides moderation, anti-spam, suspicious-link detection, event logging, and server configuration features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot processes, why it is processed, and how users and server administrators can request information about or deletion of stored data.

By using the Bot, server administrators and users acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Policy.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the Bot's features enabled on a Discord server, the Bot may process the following information:

- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and moderator IDs where necessary for Bot functionality.
- **Moderation records:** warnings, moderation actions, reasons provided by moderators, timestamps, and related identifiers.
- **Server configuration:** settings required for logging, anti-spam protection, link filtering, and other administrative features.
- **Message information:** message content and related metadata may be temporarily inspected to detect spam or suspicious links. Message content or related event details may also appear in server logs when message-edit or message-deletion logging is enabled.
- **Join events:** information needed to identify newly created accounts and record relevant join events.

The Bot is not intended to collect personal information unrelated to its Discord functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed for the following purposes:

- Providing moderation commands and maintaining warning records.
- Detecting spam, repeated messages, and suspicious links.
- Recording moderation actions and configured message events.
- Identifying potentially suspicious account-join activity.
- Saving server-specific Bot settings.
- Troubleshooting, maintaining, and securing the Bot.

The Bot does not use this information for advertising or sell personal information.

## 4. Storage and Retention

Moderation records and server settings may be stored in an SQLite database. Anti-spam tracking data may be held temporarily in memory.

Logging features may send relevant event information to a Discord channel configured by a server administrator. Information posted in a Discord channel is also subject to Discord's systems and the server's access permissions.

Information stored in a database may remain there until it is deleted by the Bot operator or through an implemented deletion procedure. Temporary in-memory tracking data may be lost when the Bot restarts.

No fixed retention period is promised by this Policy. Server administrators should not assume that deleting a Discord message automatically deletes copies of information already recorded in a moderation log or database.

## 5. Disclosure and Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and uses Discord's API. Information transmitted through Discord is subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

Information recorded in server log channels may be visible to members who have permission to access those channels.

The Bot operator does not sell personal information or disclose it to third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Bot and its operator against abuse or security incidents.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information and access to the Bot's infrastructure. However, no software system or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring appropriate permissions for channels receiving Bot logs.
